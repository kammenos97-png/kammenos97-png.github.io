This policy explains what information the mobile game Sand Sort Puzzle (package com.kammenos.sandsort) uses, why, and the choices you have. The game is published on Google Play by the independent developer Kammenos ("we").

Information the game stores on your device

The game saves your progress on your phone only: completed levels, coins (gold), hearts, settings such as sound and language, and timers used to limit how many ads you see. This data is not sent to us. We do not run our own servers, and the game has no accounts, chat or sign-in.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version)
  • ad interactions (ads shown, taps, completed video ads) and diagnostic data such as crash and performance logs

Google uses this data to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance and prevent fraud. If you consent, ads may be personalised. Google's practices are described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested. You can accept or refuse personalised ads. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with the Privacy settings button in the game's settings. If you refuse, you may still see non-personalised ads.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your phone's Android settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Google › Ads, depending on the device).
  • Delete all game data stored on your phone by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage in Android settings.
  • Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we do not hold personal data ourselves, requests about ad data are handled by Google; we will help you with any request sent to the address below.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Security and retention

Game progress stays on your device until you delete it. Data collected by Google AdMob is kept according to Google's own retention policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes, for example if new features or services are added. The effective date at the top will show the latest version.
